# Changelog

## [0.1.0]

- Controller feedback for Fallout New Vegas 1.4.0.525 with xNVSE 6.4.9, ShowOff 1.84, and the x86 InputNext XInput bundle.
- HD haptics with classic rumble fallback for weapon fire, hits, reloads, movement, and confirmed actions such as lockpicking and terminal hacking. Includes profiles derived from New Vegas sounds.
- Independent aim and attack trigger resistance with shot recoil. Attack resistance releases when the magazine empties.
- Configurable health, radiation, and stealth lightbar feedback.
