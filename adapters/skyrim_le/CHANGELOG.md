# Changelog

## [0.1.0]

- Native x86 integration for Skyrim Classic and Legendary Edition with SKSE 1.7.3.
- Body feedback and health lightbar indications through the shared Skyrim policy.
- Reduced the audio bank from 46 to 18 supported profiles without changing retained audio records. Removed unavailable settings from the shipped INI and reference; older body and trigger settings remain accepted.
- Controller, menu and focus handling with Classic-specific native bindings.
