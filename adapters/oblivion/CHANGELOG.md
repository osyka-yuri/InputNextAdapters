# Changelog

## [0.1.0]

- Controller feedback for Oblivion Classic 1.2.0.416 with xOBSE 22.15. Use the x86 InputNext DirectInput bundle.
- HD haptics with classic rumble fallback for combat, archery, magic, and confirmed actions such as picking locks and crafting. Includes profiles derived from Oblivion's sounds.
- Adaptive trigger resistance for blocking and drawing a bow, plus lightbar feedback for health. Optional movement cues and per-effect settings are available in the INI.
- The Options button can open the pause menu through InputNext.
